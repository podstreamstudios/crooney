Campbell River · The conditions of emergence

The same river.
Different angles.
Thirty years.

The Campbell River runs through the research the way it runs through the landscape — it doesn't start where you're standing. Its conditions were set upstream, long before your arrival.

George Crooney performs at Campbell River Lodge beside the same water where Roderick Haig-Brown theorized the ethics of reading a river, and where Bing Crosby fished in the crooner tradition that George Crooney inhabits today.

The convergence was not manufactured. It was found. That's what conditions of emergence look like in practice.
