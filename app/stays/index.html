The opportunity

A location that books the accommodation.

Film crews scouting the North Island through INFilm.ca are already looking for locations with the qualities Campbell River offers — wild rivers, old-growth forests, dramatic coastline, and the cultural heritage of one of BC's most storied fishing destinations.

The gap: location scouting and crew accommodation are currently handled as separate problems. A crew that finds their location on the North Island still needs to find somewhere for 20–80 people to stay, eat, and work for weeks at a time.

Campbell River Lodge is not just a hotel near the locations. It is a location. The outdoor riverbank, the Goose & Gander, the dock, the mountain backdrop — these are production assets that also happen to sleep and feed a full crew.

The channel this page proposes: INFilm.ca surfaces the North Island as a film destination → Campbell River Lodge captures the accommodation booking → a commission flows to the connector who made the introduction.

Campbell River Lodge

Campbell River, BC · Goose & Gander

On the banks of the Campbell River — the same river Roderick Haig-Brown wrote about and Bing Crosby fished. The Lodge is a full-service hospitality property with outdoor riverbank space, event catering, and enough accommodation to house a production crew.

The outdoor terrace, dock access, mountain backdrop, and old-growth river setting are production assets. A full-property buyout for a film crew is a viable and significant revenue event for the Lodge.
